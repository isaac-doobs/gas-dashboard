This app needs your private key once.

  1. On a computer, open your Thermostat & Gas sheet.
  2. Choose Thermostat → Set up phone app (QR)…
  3. Scan the code with the iPhone camera and open it in Safari.

Key received. Now install the app:

  1. Tap Share in Safari.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, keep the name Gas, tap Add.
  3. Open Gas from your Home Screen. It runs fullscreen.

If the Home Screen app still asks for the key, tap Copy key, open the app and paste it there.
